📖 21 Card Rummy Guide & Rules

Table rules, card groups, jokers, honors points, and declaration requirements

🎴 Game Essentials, Initial Deal & Goal

21-Card Rummy (also known as Marriage Rummy) is played with 3 standard 52-card decks plus 3 Printed Jokers (Poochies, 1 per deck), totaling 159 cards. Initially, 21 cards are dealt to each player, one Cut Card is revealed to establish the hand's Jokers and Honors, and one open card begins the Discard Pile.

Your Goal: Either be the first player to Declare (Show) your entire hand validly grouped (to win the round with 0 penalty points and collect Honors), or minimize your penalty points if another player declares first or your hand is weak (by forming protected groups or choosing to Scoot early).

🧭 High-Level Flow of a Hand

  1. Cards are dealt to each player: Every seated player receives 21 cards face-down. A Cut Card is revealed under the Stock Deck to define Jokers/Honors, and one open card starts the Discard Pile.
  2. Arrange cards & evaluate chances of winning: Sort and organize your cards into stands to spot Pure Sequences, Sequences, Sets, Doubles, and Honors, then decide whether to play for a Show or play defensively to keep points low.
  3. On your turn, choose to Scoot or pick up a card: Before drawing, you may Scoot (Drop) to leave the round with a fixed drop penalty (0 pts for Free Scoot), or Draw 1 card from either the face-down Stock Deck or the top of the Discard Pile.
  4. Organize & Discard (or Declare): Use your extra card to improve your groups, then either tap Show / Declare if all cards meet the winning requirements or Discard 1 unneeded card back down to your hand size to pass the turn.
  5. Grouping & Point Settlement: Turns continue clockwise until a player declares a valid Show. Other active players arrange their best valid groups to reduce Deadwood, and Penalty Points + Honors are settled for the round.

🧮 What Points Are & How They Are Calculated

There are two kinds of points tracked in every round: Penalty Points (lower is better — high points hurt you or can eliminate you in Tournament mode) and Honors / Maal Points (higher is better — bonus points collected by the round winner).

  • Card Values for Penalty Points (Deadwood): Face cards & Aces (A, K, Q, J, 10) = 10 pts each; number cards (2–9) = face value (2 to 9 pts); all Jokers (Joker, Pukka Joker, Poochie, Paplu, Taplu) = 0 penalty pts.
  • If a player Declares (Winner): Pays 0 penalty points for the round.
  • If you Scoot / Drop before drawing: Fixed penalty of 30 pts (First Drop / Scoot) or 70 pts (Middle Drop), or 0 pts for a qualifying Free Scoot / Free Game.
  • If another player Declares first (Deadwood Calculation): Any cards not in protected groups are added together up to the 120-point cap. If you formed 3+ Pure Sequences (or 7+ Doubles), all valid groups are protected (only loose cards count); otherwise, only Jokers, 4+ card Pure Runs, and Tunnelas are protected.
  • Honors (Maal) Settlement: Holding Cut-suit specials (Marriages, Pukka Jokers, Poochies, Paplus, Taplus) earns Honors points. Under Winner Takes Honors, each paying loser also pays the winner's Honors total.

🔄 Your Turn Action Details

  • 1. Draw: On your turn, draw 1 card from either the face-down Stock Deck or the face-up Discard Pile (or Scoot/Drop before drawing).
  • 2. Group & Organize: Arrange your hand into Stands (1, 2, or 3 stands). Tap any card to open the Move Pad or drag & drop cards directly between stands.
  • 3. Discard: Discard 1 card to the Discard Pile to complete your turn.

🔒 Joker Pickup Rule (Discard Restriction)

Discarded Jokers CANNOT be picked up from the discard pile!

  • If a player discards any Joker (Joker, Pukka Joker, Paplu, Taplu, or Poochie), opponents cannot draw it. The card is marked with 🔒 and you must draw from the Stock deck instead.
  • Exception Initial Open Card: If the very first card turned face-up from the deck at the beginning of the round is a Joker, the first player is permitted to pick it up!

✅ Valid Show Requirements

To declare a winning Show, all 21 cards must be arranged into valid groups with:

  • Mandatory At least 3 Pure Sequences: A sequence of 3 or more consecutive cards of the same suit without Joker substitution.
  • Special Groups that count as a Pure Sequence in this game:
    • Natural Suited Run: 3+ consecutive cards in one suit. Note: A natural sequence can include a joker card, as long as it is played in its natural suit and number (e.g. if 5♠ is the Pukka Joker, 5♠ - 6♠ - 7♠ is a pure sequence; 5♥ - 6♥ - 7♥ is also a pure sequence).
    • Marriage Trio: Holding Taplu + Pukka Joker + Paplu in the cut suit (e.g. 6♦ - 7♦ - 8♦) qualifies as a Pure Sequence!
    • Three Poochies: 3 Printed Jokers (PJ★) grouped together qualifies as a Pure Sequence!
    • Tunnela: Exactly 3 identical cards of the same rank and same suit (e.g. K♥ - K♥ - K♥) qualifies as a Pure Sequence!
  • Remaining Groups: All other cards must be arranged into valid Sequences (pure or using Jokers), Sets (3 or 4 cards of same rank in distinct suits), or Tunnelas.
  • Auto-Grouping Priority: When auto-grouping upon declare, Pure Sequences always take precedence over Sets (e.g. if 9♠ can participate in an 8♠-9♠-10♠ pure sequence or a 9♠-9♦-9♣ set, it will prioritize the pure sequence).

👑 The Cut Card & Honors (Maal) Roles

At the start of each hand, one card is cut from the deck. Its rank and suit establish all Jokers and Honors points:

PUKKA JOKER Exact rank & suit of Cut Card (remaining after marriages) Tiered: 1 = 20 pts • 2 = 40 pts (+20 each after)
POOCHIE Printed Jokers (PJ★) — 3 total in the deck Tiered: 1 = 20 pts • 2 = 50 pts • 3 = 70 pts (+20 each after)
PAPLU Same suit as Cut Card, 1 rank higher (+1 rank) Tiered Pool: 1 = 10 pts • 2 = 30 pts • 3 = 50 pts (+10 each after)
TAPLU Same suit as Cut Card, 1 rank lower (-1 rank) Tiered Pool: 1 = 10 pts • 2 = 30 pts • 3 = 50 pts (+10 each after)
JOKER Same rank as Cut Card in other 3 suits (e.g. 7♠, 7♥, 7♣) Free wild card in groups (0 honors pts)

💍 Marriage Details

A Marriage is formed by holding Taplu + Pukka Joker + Paplu together of the cut suit in your hand (e.g. 6♦ + 7♦ + 8♦ when 7♦ is cut).

  • Each completed Marriage trio awards 50 points.
  • Any cards left over after assembling Marriages are scored in their individual categories (Pukka Joker: 20 pts each; Paplu & Taplu: tiered pool).
  • A Marriage group also counts as a Pure Sequence for declaration!

🚪 Shoots / Scoots & Drop Penalties

If dealt an unpromising hand, you may choose to scoot or drop before drawing a card:

ActionConditionPenalty
Free Scoot Dealt 6 Doubles before drawing a card 0 points & 0 honors
Free Game Holding 7 Doubles after drawing a card 0 points & 0 honors
Scoot (First Drop) Drop out before drawing any card on your first turn 30 points
Middle Drop Drop out before drawing on any subsequent turn 70 points
Forfeit / Abandon Leaving table mid-hand after a forfeit 100 points
Max Deadwood Deadwood cap for ungrouped cards 120 points

🛡️ Deadwood Protection Rules

  • If you have ≥ 3 Pure Sequences or ≥ 7 Doubles: All valid grouped cards (pure sequences, impure sequences, sets, tunnelas, and double pairs) are protected (0 deadwood). Only loose cards count as deadwood.
  • If you have < 3 Pure Sequences: ONLY Jokers, Pure Sequences of 4 or more cards, and Tunnelas are protected! All other cards (including 3-card pure runs, sets, impure runs, and loose cards) count as deadwood up to the 120-point maximum.
  • Card values: Face cards (A, K, Q, J, 10) = 10 pts; number cards = face value (2 to 9).

💰 Winner Takes Honors Settlement

Under our table rules, Winner Takes Honors:

  • The winning player collects their full accumulated Honors (Maal) points from paying losing players at the table.
  • Free Scoot / Free Game players: Owe 0 penalty points and 0 honors.
  • Dropped / Scooted players pay: Drop penalty (30 pts for Scoot or 70 pts for Middle Drop) + Winner's Honors points.
  • Non-dropped losing players pay: Deadwood score (up to 120 pts) + Winner's Honors points.
  • The winner receives: (Number of paying losers) × (Winner's Honors points).

📚 Rummy Terminology Glossary

Every key term used at the table and in the rules, explained clearly:

Cut Card / Cut Joker

The card revealed from the deck at the start of each hand. Its rank and suit determine who becomes the Pukka Joker, Jokers, Paplu, and Taplu for that round.

Deadwood Penalty Pts

The penalty point sum of ungrouped or unprotected cards remaining in your hand when another player declares (A/K/Q/J/10 = 10 pts, 2–9 = face value, Jokers = 0 pts), capped at 120 pts by default.

Declare / Show Win Round

Submitting a complete, valid hand—all dealt cards grouped with the required Pure Sequences (plus 1 final discard after drawing)—to win the hand with 0 penalty points.

Discard Pile

The face-up pile where players throw 1 unwanted card to finish a turn. Opponents may draw the top card unless it is a 🔒 locked Joker.

Double (Pair)

Two identical cards of the exact same rank and suit across the multi-deck shoe (e.g., two K♠s). Holding 7 dealt or 8 drawn Doubles qualifies for a Doubles Show.

Drop (First / Middle)

Folding at the start of your turn before drawing. A First Drop (Scoot) costs 30 pts; a Middle Drop (after taking at least one turn) costs 70 pts.

Free Scoot / Free Game

Exiting a round with 0 penalty points and 0 Honors paid when dealt 6 Doubles before drawing (Free Scoot) or holding 7 Doubles after drawing (Free Game).

Grouping Mode

The interactive organizer opened when you tap Show / Declare or when an opponent declares, letting you lock in Pure Sequences, Runs, and Sets to minimize Deadwood.

Honors (Maal) Bonus Pts

Special bonus points awarded for Cut-suit specials (Marriage, Pukka Joker, Poochie, Paplu, Taplu). Under Winner Takes Honors, paying losers pay the winner's Honors total.

Impure Sequence

A run of 3+ consecutive same-suit cards where one or more missing cards are filled in by a Joker (e.g., 4♥ - 5♥ - Joker), or a group of 3+ Jokers.

Joker Wild Card

Cards matching the Cut Card's rank in the other 3 suits (0 Honors pts), as well as Printed Poochies and Pukka Jokers. Can substitute for another card inside an Impure Sequence or Set. Worth 0 Deadwood pts; thrown Jokers are 🔒 locked.

Marriage 50 pts

Holding the trio of Taplu + Pukka Joker + Paplu together in the Cut Suit (e.g., 6♦ - 7♦ - 8♦ when 7♦ is Cut). Counts as both 50 Honors pts and a Pure Sequence.

Paplu +1 Cut Suit

The card in the Cut Card's exact suit one rank higher (+1 rank, wrapping K → A). It earns tiered Honors (Maal) points and combines with Pukka Joker + Taplu to form a Marriage.

Poochie (Printed Joker)

Printed Joker cards (PJ★) included in the packs. Acts as a wild card in groups and awards tiered Honors points. Grouping 3 Poochies together counts as a Pure Sequence.

Pukka Joker (Tiplu)

Cards matching BOTH the exact rank and suit of the Cut Card. Acts as a wild card and earns tiered Honors points (20 / 40+ pts).

Pure Sequence Required

3 or more consecutive cards of the same suit with NO wild substitution (e.g., 8♣ - 9♣ - 10♣). At least 3 Pure Sequences are required by default to Declare.

Scoot

Voluntarily folding on your turn before drawing a card to cap your loss (30 pts on your first turn, 70 pts Middle Drop) rather than risking up to 120 Deadwood pts.

Set (Trial)

3 or 4 cards of the same rank in different suits (e.g., Q♠ - Q♥ - Q♦), optionally using Jokers to fill a suit.

Stand

One of the organizer trays holding cards in your hand. Moving cards between Stands keeps your hand tidy without affecting your turn.

Stock Deck

The face-down draw deck in the center of the table. You may always draw from the Stock Deck on your turn, especially when the Discard is a 🔒 locked Joker.

Taplu (Nichlu) -1 Cut Suit

The card in the Cut Card's exact suit one rank lower (-1 rank, wrapping A → K). Shares the Paplu/Taplu Honors pool and completes a Marriage with Pukka Joker + Paplu.

Tunnela

Three identical cards of the exact same rank and suit across the decks (e.g., three 9♠s). Always protected from Deadwood, counts as a Pure Sequence, and 3 Tunnelas wins instantly.
