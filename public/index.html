Scoring Rules:
  • Marriage (50 pts): 1 Tuplu + 1 Cut Joker + 1 Paplu together in the cut card's suit.
  • Cut Joker (20 pts each): Joker card matching both rank and suit of the cut card.
  • Poochie (Tiered): Printed joker — 1 = 20 pts, 2 = 50 pts, 3 = 70 pts (+20 each).
  • Paplu / Tuplu (Tiered pool): Same suit as cut card (+1 rank and -1 rank) remaining after marriages — 1 = 10 pts, 2 = 30 pts, 3 = 50 pts.
  • Settlement & Drops: Every loser—including players who drop/scoot (30 pts drop penalty)—must pay the winner's total Maal points.
